# Design direction — Rishwat Mukt Bharat

## Source direction
**Extension of the supplied bribery portal HTML.** The blueprint and source establish the final direction: a serious, trustworthy Indian public-service interface that feels like a civic information desk rather than a generic startup dashboard.

## Design movement
- Calm civic utility with clear signposting and restrained hierarchy.
- Public-service editorial density: the page should answer “where do I go next?” without hiding the evidence.
- Human-readable, multilingual-ready, respectful of sensitive reporting.

## Core principles
1. **Clarity before conversion:** routing, urgency, and next steps are always visible.
2. **Trust through provenance:** recommendations carry a workbook-backed source note and official-site verification reminder.
3. **Dignity under stress:** short labels, direct validation, no playful motion, no blame.
4. **Progressive disclosure:** show the primary complaint path first; reveal model settings and directory metadata only when useful.
5. **Safe defaults:** no raw OTP in logs/client source, no external destination invented by AI, anonymous reporting remains available.

## Visual system
- **Palette:** deep navy `#14285a` / `#0d1b3e` for authority; saffron `#e8781a` for action and focus; green `#128807` for confirmed routing; warm paper `#f3f5f9` / white cards; red only for urgent/errors.
- **Type:** Noto Serif for h1–h3; Noto Sans for UI/body; fallback Georgia/system stacks.
- **Layout:** max-width 1140px; 8px-ish spacing rhythm; two-column desktop grids collapsing to one column under 820px; sharp 4–6px cards and borders.
- **Interaction:** short 150–220ms transitions; no decorative parallax; visible focus rings; status messages use `aria-live`.
- **Signature elements:** tricolor top rule, left saffron borders, navy sticky nav, route cards with green confirmation, stepper form, “not official website” ribbon.

## Brand essence and voice
- Name: **Rishwat Mukt Bharat**.
- Voice: direct, non-judgmental, procedural, protective.
- Repeated disclaimer: “Prototype portal for demonstration — not an official Government of India website.”

## Logo
A distinct flat civic mark will use a navy circular compass/registry symbol with saffron and green registration ticks, no text in the mark, and an opaque square favicon variant. The header may use the same SVG mark at a readable size.
